⏪️ R&B Rewind

Whitney Houston, “I'm Your Baby Tonight” (1990)

36 years ago this week, on November 6th, 1990, Whitney dropped I'm Your Baby Tonight. It was her third album and her most R&B-leaning one yet, with L.A. Reid and Babyface behind the boards and appearances from Luther Vandross and Stevie Wonder.

The title track hit #1 on the Hot 100 and spent two weeks at #1 on the R&B chart. The album topped the R&B albums chart too.

Did you know 🧠

This album was a response. After Whitney was booed (crazy!) at the 1989 Soul Train Music Awards and called “not black enough,” she went after a funkier sound on purpose. She picked L.A. Reid and Babyface because of their work on Bobby Brown's Don't Be Cruel. According to Reid, she nailed the title track in one take, in under an hour.

📡 R&B Radar

Mýa, “Saturday Night” (2026)

Mýa is in full throwback mode. “Saturday Night” comes from Retrospect, her first album in eight years, a love letter to 70s and 80s funk and soul that she released on her own label, Planet 9.

The video dropped on her birthday and turns the clock back to a late-80s house party.

Did you know 🧠

There's not a single sample on Retrospect. Mýa skipped them on purpose, following advice she got from Prince.

🗓️ On This Day

On This Day, November 4th, 2016

10 years ago today, Alicia Keys released Here, her sixth album and her first in four years. 🎹

It debuted at #2 on the Billboard 200 and gave Alicia her seventh #1 on the R&B albums chart.

Did you know 🧠

Alicia has said she wrote around 30 songs for Here in about 10 days. Her pregnancy delayed the release, so the album sat for a while before finally coming out in 2016.
