⏪️ R&B Rewind

Dru Hill, “How Deep Is Your Love” (1998)

28 years ago this week, on October 27th, 1998, Dru Hill dropped Enter the Dru. The album went 2x platinum, peaked at #2 on the Billboard 200 and gave us “How Deep Is Your Love,” “These Are the Times” and “Beauty.”

“How Deep Is Your Love” became the group's biggest hit, reaching #3 on the Hot 100 and holding #1 on the R&B chart for three weeks.

Did you know 🧠

The song was made for the Rush Hour soundtrack. Rush Hour director Brett Ratner shot the video on a rooftop in Hong Kong, and it's mixed with clips from the movie. Bonus: the album title is a nod to the Bruce Lee classic Enter the Dragon.

📡 R&B Radar

NAO, “Fly Away” (2026)

London's own NAO surprised everyone this month with Autumn, a brand-new album she announced just days before it dropped on October 16th. “Fly Away” is the standout.

Autumn is NAO's first independent album after four records with RCA. It's also the first of four seasonal albums in a series she's calling “A Soulful Suite,” and it leans into the 90s and 2000s neo-soul she grew up on.

Did you know 🧠

Before her solo career took off, NAO studied jazz in London and spent six years singing in an all-female a cappella group. She's also a Grammy nominee and co-wrote Ariana Grande's “Be Alright.”

🗓️ On This Day

On This Day, October 28th, 1987

Happy birthday to Frank Ocean, born on this day in Long Beach, California. 🎈

Before he was Frank Ocean, he was writing songs for Brandy, John Legend, Beyoncé and Justin Bieber. His debut album Channel Orange (2012) went on to win the Grammy for Best Urban Contemporary Album.

Did you know 🧠

“Thinkin Bout You” wasn't originally meant for Frank. He wrote it as a demo for singer Bridget Kelly, who recorded her own version called “Thinking About Forever.” Frank's version leaked in 2011, and the rest is history.
