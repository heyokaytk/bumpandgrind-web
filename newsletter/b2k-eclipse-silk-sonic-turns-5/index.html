⏪️ R&B Rewind

Silk Sonic, “Smokin Out The Window” (2021)

5 years ago this week, on November 12th, 2021, Bruno Mars and Anderson .Paak dropped An Evening with Silk Sonic.

It debuted at #2 on the Billboard 200, and “Leave the Door Open” went to #1 on the Hot 100 before winning four Grammys, including Record and Song of the Year.

Did you know 🧠

“Smokin Out the Window” started as a backstage joke between Bruno and Anderson, and they turned it into a hook. The funk legend Bootsy Collins named the duo and also hosts the album. That's his voice you hear on the intro.

Watch Silk Sonic, “Smokin Out The Window”

📡 R&B Radar

B2K, Eclipse (2026)

Omarion, J-Boog, Raz-B and Lil Fizz are back with Eclipse, their first album since 2002's Pandemonium! (the album that gave you “Bump, Bump, Bump”). It's 12 tracks from a more grown-up B2K, led by the singles “Mileage” and “Lil Freak.”

Did you know 🧠

Bow Wow joins them on a track called “Boyfriend,” a nod to “Girlfriend,” his 2007 hit with Omarion. The album also comes off the back of their 25th anniversary tour earlier this year.

Watch B2K, “Boyfriend” (ft. Bow Wow)

🗓️ On This Day

On This Day, November 11th, 2008

18 years ago today, T-Pain released Thr33 Ringz, his third album. It debuted at #4 on the Billboard 200, went to #1 on the R&B/Hip-Hop albums chart, and gave us “Can't Believe It” with Lil Wayne and “Freeze” with Chris Brown.

Did you know 🧠

Thr33 Ringz was nominated for Best Contemporary R&B Album at the Grammys, but it lost to Beyoncé's I Am... Sasha Fierce.

Watch T-Pain, “Can't Believe It” (ft. Lil Wayne)
