NYE questions

Good to know

Can I buy tickets at the door?

No. This one is ticketed in advance only, and there are just 100.

Do I get into the after party?

Yes. Every ticket includes fast-track entry to the after party at The Toothy Moose.

How does the season pass work?

The first 50 tickets sold each include a 2027 season pass, which gets you free cover to every Bump & Grind event next year. More on the season pass.

Can I have dinner at Lot Six before the party?

Yes. Lot Six takes dinner reservations on New Year's Eve. Book a table on OpenTable. Dinner is booked separately, so you'll still need a party ticket.

Can I get a refund?

All sales are final. No refunds or exchanges.

What music will be playing?
